What is Mauli Auto Parts?

Mauli Auto Parts is a billing and stock management app for auto parts shops, two-wheeler and four-wheeler spare parts dealers, and garages.

The shop enters its parts once. After that, every bill reduces the stock automatically, so the shop always knows what is available, what is running low and what to order. All data is stored on the shop's own phone, and it can be backed up to the shop owner's own Google Drive.

How Google Drive backup works

It is optional. Billing always works without it.

  • When you tap Connect Google Drive in the app, Google asks for your permission. The app only requests access to files it creates itself (Google's drive.file permission). It cannot see or open any other file in your Google Drive.
  • The app creates a folder named "Mauli Auto Parts Backup" in your Drive and saves a backup there once a day, or whenever you tap Back up now. Only the latest 15 backups are kept.
  • To show which account is connected, the app reads the email address of that Google account.
  • Backups go straight from your phone to your own Google Drive. We do not run any server, and we never receive or store your data.
  • You can disconnect at any time in the app (Settings → Google Drive backup → Disconnect), or remove the app's access from your Google Account.
