Privacy Policy

Mauli Auto Parts app · Effective date: 24 October 2026

In short: your shop's data stays on your own phone. If you turn on Google Drive backup, backups go to your own Google Drive. We have no server, show no ads, use no tracking, and never sell or receive your data.

1. About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how the Mauli Auto Parts app ("the App") handles information. The App helps auto parts shops and garages make bills, manage stock and keep business records. "We" means Mauli Auto Parts, the maker of the App. "You" means the shop or person using the App.

2. Information stored on your device

When you use the App, you may enter:

  • Shop details: shop name, address, phone number, GSTIN and UPI ID.
  • Parts and stock: names, part numbers, prices, quantities and suppliers.
  • Bills and estimates: items, amounts, discounts, payment mode and pending (udhaar) amounts.
  • Customer details you choose to add: name, mobile number, vehicle number, model and KM reading.
  • App settings, such as the selected Bluetooth printer.

This information is saved only in the App's private storage on your device. We do not run any server, and we cannot see or access this information. The App contains no advertising, analytics or tracking tools.

Depending on your phone's settings, Android may also include the App's data in your device's own Google backup (Settings → Google → Backup). This is an Android feature that you control.

3. Google user data (Google Drive backup)

Google Drive backup is optional. It is used only if you tap Connect Google Drive in the App and allow access on Google's permission screen.

What the App accesses

  • Only files the App creates in your Google Drive, using Google's limited drive.file permission. The App creates a folder named "Mauli Auto Parts Backup" and saves backup files in it. It can list, read and delete only these files. It cannot see or open any other file in your Drive.
  • The email address of the connected Google account, read through the Google Drive API, so the App can show which account the backups go to.

How the App uses it

  • To save backups of your App data to your Google Drive, automatically once a day or when you tap Back up now.
  • To show your list of backups and restore one when you choose to.
  • To delete older backups so that only the latest 15 are kept.
  • To show the connected email address in the App's Settings.

Google user data is not used for advertising, is not sold, and is not used to develop, improve or train artificial intelligence or machine-learning models.

Storage and sharing

Backups are stored only in your own Google Drive. Data travels directly between your device and Google over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. We do not receive, store or share your Google user data with anyone. The connected email address is saved only on your device.

Removing access and deleting backups

  • Disconnect in the App: Settings → Google Drive backup → Disconnect.
  • Remove the App's access from your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/connections.
  • Delete backups by deleting the "Mauli Auto Parts Backup" folder from your Google Drive.

Mauli Auto Parts' use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Permissions the App uses

  • Bluetooth / Nearby devices: to find your paired bill printer and print bills.
  • Internet: only for Google Drive backup and restore. Billing works without internet.
  • Files you choose: when you save or open a backup file or an Excel (CSV) file, Android's file picker gives the App access only to the file you select.

The App does not use your location, contacts, camera or microphone.

5. Sharing bills

When you tap WhatsApp, Share, A4 / PDF or Print, the bill goes only to the app, printer or person you choose. Those apps and services have their own privacy policies.

6. Your customers' information

The shop decides which customer details to enter. The shop is responsible for collecting them fairly, with the customer's knowledge, and handling them as required by applicable law.

7. Deleting your data

You can delete bills and parts inside the App. Uninstalling the App deletes all data it stored on that device. Backups in Google Drive stay there until you delete them.

8. Security

Your data is kept in the App's private storage, which other apps cannot read. We recommend using a screen lock on your phone and keeping Google Drive backup turned on.

9. Children

The App is made for businesses and is not directed at children.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
